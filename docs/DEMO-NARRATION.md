# Demo narration

Computer-generated narration using the Mac's offline voice. Footage is actual local app playback and inspection; browser audio is muted. No human participant is represented; footage is the local production build of the same code deployed at https://finishline-race.vercel.app. Caption timings are proportional sentence estimates; review them before public upload.

## Landing

Finish Line. Same checkout, six ways through. This demo uses computer generated narration. Six real Chromium browsers race one journey: mouse, keyboard, screen reader, voice control, four hundred percent zoom and a budget phone. The track is drawn from a real recording, and the terminal prints the same results.

## The race

These are actual recorded browser measurements on a controlled bakery fixture, not an AI builder benchmark. Four of six lanes finish. The screen reader and voice lanes stop at real barriers. Counts are measured. The human time clock is an estimate.

## The evidence

Each lane opens its evidence. The screen reader lane reached a button without a usable name. You can inspect the actual browser frame, its announcement and the event trail. The fix prompt comes from a closed reason code. Nothing here is a human screen reader session, and a finished lane is not a certificate of accessibility.

## Correctness

The engine has a published correctness matrix. Ten deliberately isolated fixtures, six lanes each. All sixty expectations pass, including focus traps, missing names, mismatched labels, occlusion and reflow. Unknown engine failures are kept separate from accessibility failures. The method also publishes its limits and timing assumptions.

## The repair

This is the repaired controlled bakery. It follows the same three step journey: add the first product, open the cart, then checkout. The dated rerun finishes all six lanes. Both versions retain their real timelines and screenshots. We do not attribute these hand authored fixtures to any AI builder.

## Your journey
